Frequently Asked Questions

Bigg Boss 20 Voting & Eviction FAQs

Who is currently leading Bigg Boss Season 20 voting today?

🔥 CURRENT TREND: Qazi Touqeer (1,38,500) holds a narrow lead over ScoutOP (1,32,200), Gullu (1,26,800), and Yung DSA (1,20,900) in our fan sentiment tracker. Public sentiment continues to evolve dynamically as weekend 'Weekend Ka Vaar' approaches.

How many times can I vote for Bigg Boss 20 on JioCinema?

On the official JioCinema platform, users are permitted to submit 1 vote per registered phone number/ID per voting cycle. On VisitBest's public sentiment poll, you can cast your opinion vote and check real-time trends every 24 hours.

When does Bigg Boss 20 voting start and end each week?

Official voting begins Monday night at 10:30 PM IST immediately after the weekly nomination episode finishes airing, and remains open until Friday night at 11:59 PM IST. Votes cast after midnight are not counted.

Are missed call voting numbers completely free?

Yes, all numbers starting with 1800-313-xxxx are 100% toll-free across all telecom networks in India (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL). Your balance will not be deducted.

How is the weekly eviction decided in Bigg Boss Season 20?

The contestant who receives the lowest total verified votes across JioCinema and missed calls among the nominated housemates is eliminated during the Sunday Weekend Ka Vaar episode hosted by Salman Khan.
