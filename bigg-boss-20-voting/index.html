Bigg Boss 20 Week 4 Voting & Eviction FAQs

Who is leading the Bigg Boss 20 voting poll today?

Qazi Touqeer is currently leading the VisitBest audience poll with 138,500 votes, followed closely by ScoutOP (132,200), Gullu (126,800), and Yung DSA (120,900). The rankings represent VisitBest audience-poll activity and should not be confused with official Bigg Boss voting figures.

Is Gullu nominated this week?

No. Gullu is the current house captain and was not included in the Week 4 nomination task. He is currently safe from this week's nomination list and cannot be evicted this weekend.

Which contestants are nominated for eviction in Week 4?

11 contestants are nominated this week: Qazi Touqeer, ScoutOP, Yung DSA, Aman Gandhi, Amrapali Dubey, Kanika Mann, Love Gill, Mahhi Vij, Mary Kom, Rhiti Tiwari, and Uditi Singh.

Who is safe from nomination in Week 4?

Three contestants are safe this week: Gullu (protected by captaincy immunity), Arishfa Khan, and Aasif Khan.

How many times can I vote for Bigg Boss 20 on JioCinema?

On the official JioCinema streaming platform, users are permitted to submit votes per registered Indian mobile number during each active weekly voting cycle. On VisitBest's public sentiment poll, you can cast your opinion vote daily to track public sentiment.

When does Bigg Boss 20 voting start and end each week?

Official voting begins Monday night at 10:30 PM IST immediately after the weekly nomination episode finishes airing, and remains open until Friday night at 11:59 PM IST.
