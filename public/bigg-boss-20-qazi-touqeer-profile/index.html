The Fame Gurukul connection

Qazi won Fame Gurukul with Ruprekha Banerjee in 2005. He is associated with Srinagar and a career in singing. His return to mainstream reality television in Season 20 explains the renewed interest in that earlier competition.

The two shows ask different things from a contestant. Fame Gurukul centered on a music competition. Bigg Boss places participants in a shared house, where everyday choices become part of the competition.

Why an older fan base is context, not a result

Recognition can bring attention to a participant before a new episode airs. It cannot tell viewers which decisions that participant will make in a task. It also cannot reveal private official voting totals.

For a fair reading, keep the earlier win in the career record and assess the current episode on its own events. A memorable television past and a controversial house moment do not cancel one another out.
