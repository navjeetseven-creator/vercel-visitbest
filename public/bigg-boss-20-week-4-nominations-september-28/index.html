Bigg Boss 20 Week 4 Nominations: September 28 Report

Eleven contestants were reported nominated after the September 28, 2026 episode. This is a dated account of that round, not an undated claim about who is nominated whenever the page is opened.

By VisitBestPrepared September 30, 2026Dated archive: September 28, 2026
Dated reporting. This page describes September 28, 2026. Later episodes may change the position. The account is attributed to the linked report, not presented as first-hand viewing.

The reported list

Navbharat Times identified these eleven nominees: Qazi Touqeer, Scout, Yung DSA, Aman Gandhi, Amrapali Dubey, Kanika Mann, Love Gill, Mahhi Vij, Mary Kom, Rhiti Tiwari, Uditi Singh.

Its account listed Gullu as protected in the task, with Arishfa Khan and Aasif Khan outside the final nomination list. Later announcements can change a contestant’s status.

What changed the nomination decision

The report described a task requiring agreement within pairs. Where the participants could not agree, both could enter the nomination list. That condition put cooperation and self-selection at the center of the round.

VisitBest’s reading: a paired decision asks a different question from simply naming a rival. It forces participants to weigh their own position against a relationship. That is analysis of the reported mechanism, not a claim about anyone’s private motive.

What the list does not establish

A nomination list does not identify the eventual evictee. It does not reveal how many official votes anyone has received. It also does not make a fan-poll percentage an official survival probability.

Use the nomination explainer for the difference between eligibility and departure. The fan-versus-official guide explains how to read poll rankings.

Where to follow the next development

For the episode, use the official viewing destinations. Check the current official voting instructions before participating. This dated report does not publish an assumed closing time or missed-call numbers.
