Bigg Boss 20 Fan Polls vs Official Voting: Key Differences

A fan poll records the preferences of the people who use it. Official voting follows the show’s announced process. Clicking a contestant on VisitBest does not submit a vote to JioHotstar.

The destinations record different actions

VisitBest’s polls belong to VisitBest. They can ask who entertained viewers, who performed well or who might win. The official process belongs to the broadcaster and its designated platform, with the rules displayed for the current round.

The distinction remains even when the same names appear in both places. A poll page cannot transfer its visitors’ choices to another platform simply by calling the interaction “voting.”

What a poll percentage means

A percentage describes a share of the entries counted under that poll’s method. It does not describe every viewer of the show. Participation can depend on who sees the page, when they arrive and which fan communities share it.

A worked example: 40 selections out of 100 counted selections equal 40%. If another 100 entries arrive, the percentage can move even if those first 40 entries remain unchanged. This example explains arithmetic; it is not a live Bigg Boss 20 result.

Compare like-for-like questions

The winner-prediction poll asks for a forecast. The entertainer poll asks about entertainment. The best-contestant poll asks for an assessment of performance.

Those rankings can differ without either poll being broken. A visitor can enjoy one participant most and think another has a stronger chance of winning. Read the poll question before comparing totals.

A popularity ranking cannot confirm an eviction

A fan poll does not reveal the official platform’s private vote total. It also does not establish the rules used for a special outcome. Screenshots claiming “official leaked votes” need an identifiable official source before they can be treated as results.

VisitBest’s new guides do not publish invented eviction percentages or convert fan totals into survival probabilities. An elimination is recorded when the show announces it.

How to participate in the appropriate place

For a fan opinion, open a clearly labeled VisitBest poll and read its question. For official voting, open the current official Bigg Boss page and follow the active instructions. Verify the round, selected name and confirmation shown after submission.

If the option is absent or a submission fails, use the troubleshooting guide. Do not substitute a third-party poll for a missing official voting option.

Read any ranking with its date and method

A useful poll record gives its question, observation time and counting method. If those are absent, treat the number as an informal indication from that page, not an audited statement about the full audience.

The season guide connects the different polls and explanatory pages. It helps readers move from an opinion to the relevant source without confusing the two types of participation.

Questions about this topic

Does a VisitBest poll vote count in Bigg Boss 20?

No. VisitBest records an unofficial fan opinion. Official votes follow the show’s designated process.

Can a fan poll predict the winner?

It can record a visitor prediction, but its rankings do not confirm the winner or reveal official voting totals.
