Bigg Boss 20 September 29: Captaincy Contenders Emerge

The September 29, 2026 episode coverage linked a ration task with captaincy. Mahhi Vij and Uditi Singh were reported as contenders, leaving the distinction between reaching the contest and winning it important.

The ration task became a captaincy story

Navbharat Times reported conflict during the ration task and identified Mahhi Vij and Uditi Singh as captaincy contenders. Its account also described tension involving Qazi Touqeer and Kanika Mann, followed by an argument between Aman Gandhi and Scout.

These are reported episode developments. The page does not claim a final captain was selected or assign a penalty that the report did not establish.

VisitBest’s reading: track the consequence, not only the argument

The useful question after a task is what its result changed. Supplies, captaincy eligibility and a final house role can be separate consequences. A recap that treats them as one outcome loses that distinction.

The captaincy guide explains why “contender” needs to remain “contender” until the show announces a winner. Viewers can use that distinction when comparing later clips and headlines.

How this fits after the nomination episode

The earlier September 28 report concerns the nomination round. The September 29 coverage concerns a later task and house discussion. Combining the two into an undated status table could imply a rule change that was never announced.

Reading in sequence preserves the difference between the task that created a nomination list and a task that created captaincy contenders.
