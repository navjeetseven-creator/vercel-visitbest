Top 100 Medical Institutions in India: NIRF Ranking and Course-Check Guide

50 NIRF 2025 Medical ranked institutions plus 50 additional participants. Entries 51–100 have no published rank in this table.

What this hub covers

This hub covers medical institutions in India through one guide: a 100-entry table built from the NIRF 2025 Medical ranking and participant list. The first 50 entries are the official ranked institutions, in published rank order; the other 50 are additional participants, geographically sampled, with no published rank in this table. Because the NIRF Medical category includes institutions with differing course portfolios, this is not a list of 100 verified MBBS colleges — and each guide here follows one pattern: a stated scope and a clear label.

Directory sample or ranking? Read the label

This guide mixes both kinds of table, and the label changes halfway down. Rows 1–50 are a published ranking — each keeps its official NIRF 2025 Medical rank and score. Rows 51–100 are a participant list, not ranks 51 to 100: no rank or score is published for them here. A NIRF score is an institutional measure under that edition’s methodology, not an admission cut-off, and not evidence that every course at an institution is of the same quality.

Before you apply: five checks

  1. Start with eligibility, not ranking: confirm you meet the entrance and eligibility conditions for the exact course and academic year, and use the correct counselling authority for the seat — the guide links the MCC counselling portal and its current UG notices.
  2. Verify the exact approved course — institution, campus, qualification and year — in the NMC college and course search, recording the approved course and intake with the date checked. Never infer approval from NIRF participation, or from the word Medical in a name.
  3. Evaluate the clinical training: confirm the teaching hospital attached to the course, where clinical postings happen, and the student support provided — a medical degree is learned in wards as much as in lectures.
  4. Build the full cost for the whole course duration: read the current seat and fee notice, add hostel charges and any service or bond conditions, and leave a missing figure unresolved instead of estimating it.
  5. Confirm the language of instruction — and of the ward: check the language teaching is delivered in and the language patients at the attached hospital commonly use, because clinical learning depends on speaking with patients.

For other subjects and stages, start from the Education Guides Hub, which links the same labelled guides — schools, universities and medical institutions.

How to use the comparisons

  1. Read the source, edition and geographic limits.
  2. Shortlist feasible programmes or year groups.
  3. Save current admission, qualification and fee evidence.
  4. Resolve missing answers with the actual campus.

A directory listing, membership record and academic performance measure answer different questions. They are labelled separately throughout these guides.
