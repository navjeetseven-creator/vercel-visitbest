100 Private Schools: A Three-Country Research Guide

35 English independent, 35 Canadian CAIS and 30 Victorian non-government school records. Not a global best-school ranking.

Private-school searches become more useful when the source and geographic limits are explicit. This guide selects 100 institutions across England, Canada and the Australian state of Victoria. It uses 35 English independent-school records, 35 Canadian CAIS-directory entries and 30 Victorian non-government secondary or combined schools. The article answers the broad worldwide search topic with a defined three-country comparison. It does not claim to rank every private school in the world or to measure their results on a single scale.

Sources and selection method

Three-country sample only. Different directory definitions; no global quality rank.

Source: GIAS England, CAIS Canada and Victoria school locations. Source records checked 2026-10-01. Current admissions, courses and fees require the institution’s own current evidence. Each row links to its source.

Read what private means in each source

England’s sample uses open “Other independent school” records with age provision extending into secondary education. The Canadian component uses CAIS directory membership. Victoria uses Catholic and independent sector records classified as secondary or combined. Those definitions are related but not interchangeable. Membership, legal registration and performance are distinct evidence types. The table keeps the source visible rather than converting them into a universal quality badge.

Choose the qualification and age range

Schools can offer different awards and grade structures across the three systems. A family needs the child’s actual entry age, available subjects and route to the next stage. Combined schools may teach both younger and older pupils. Ask about the intended campus and intake. Qualification recognition depends on the receiving institution or regulator, not on the presence of a school in a private-school guide.

Examine teaching and support provision

Request a current curriculum, ordinary timetable and description of learning support. Ask how teachers communicate progress and how concerns are escalated. Language support and disability provision should be checked against the pupil’s needs. A directory cannot establish the quality of individual classrooms or the availability of specialist help. Link any inspection or accreditation claim to the correct campus and date.

Budget in the original currency

Compare tuition, deposits, uniforms, devices, activities, transport and examinations. If boarding is needed, verify that it exists and obtain a separate cost schedule. Ask about refunds and annual increases. A currency conversion should use a dated rate and stay separate from the original quoted amount. The guide avoids invented fees because the intake, grade and services can change the actual bill.

Use a documented shortlist

Reduce the table to feasible locations and qualifications. Then verify applicant eligibility, available places and written offers. For international moves, check residence and child-care arrangements through the relevant authority. Save a current source for every important claim and note unanswered questions. A clear fit between the pupil, provision and family budget is more informative than a fabricated global position.

Decision checklist

  • Read the three-country limits
  • Confirm the intended qualification
  • Check support at the campus
  • Budget in the quoted currency
  • Keep approval and membership distinct

Shortlist worksheet

For three feasible options, record the exact programme or year group, current eligibility, full cost, support provision, travel and the official evidence date. Leave a missing answer unresolved instead of estimating it.

Questions before you decide

Why only three countries?

They have usable official or membership directory records for this defined comparison. The limitation is stated openly.

Are the first entries better schools?

No. The list is sampled and ordered for navigation.

Does private mean boarding?

No. Boarding requires separate confirmation.

Does membership equal government approval?

No. Evidence types must be checked separately.

Can fees be inferred from a country?

No. Obtain the exact school, grade and intake schedule.
