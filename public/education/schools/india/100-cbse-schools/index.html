100 CBSE Schools in India: Directory and Admissions Guide

CBSE senior secondary directory sample in ten selected states/territories. No board-results rank.

A school shortlist needs the correct campus and affiliation. This guide samples 100 senior secondary records from CBSE SARAS across ten selected states and territories. Each record includes its affiliation number and official source. The table is a geographic comparison, not a claim that these are India’s 100 best schools. Similar school names can describe different branches. Families can use the record to identify the exact campus, then compare the intended grade, subject choices, learning support and travel.

Verify affiliation and branch identity

The affiliation number, district and address work together. A familiar brand can operate several separately recorded campuses. Senior secondary status does not establish every subject combination for the next intake. Families need the current curriculum and mandatory disclosures. A directory record is an identity check rather than an independent assessment of instruction, transport or safeguarding. Schools outside CBSE or the selected geography are absent by design.

Match the school to the child

The target grade, teaching language and learning needs come first. Older pupils need actual subject combinations and laboratory access. Younger pupils need reading support and a clear classroom routine. Families can ask about class size, staff continuity and the contact for additional help. Accessibility, language transition and counselling need concrete arrangements. A visit can show facilities. A written policy explains how those facilities and support are used.

Get admissions and costs in writing

The current notice needs the grade, campus and session. Application charges, admission charges and annual tuition are different costs. Transport, books, uniforms, meals and devices can add to the family bill. Refund and withdrawal terms need written confirmation. A verbal tuition quote alone is incomplete. The guide does not invent dates or fee estimates. Families can save the actual circular and the school’s response to their questions.

Inspect the daily journey and care

The commute needs to be timed at school start and finish. Families can ask who supervises transport and how delays are communicated. Handover procedures matter for younger pupils. During a visit, questions about medical response, water, toilets and reporting concerns help explain daily care. Examples of work and the assessment calendar add academic context. The best shortlist combines the right grade, manageable travel and clear evidence about support.

Decision checklist

  • Match the campus affiliation
  • Check grade and subject choices
  • Budget beyond tuition
  • Test the daily commute
  • Ask how concerns are handled

Shortlist worksheet

For three feasible options, record the exact programme or year group, current eligibility, full cost, support provision, travel and the official evidence date. Leave a missing answer unresolved instead of estimating it.

Questions before you decide

Are entries ranked by board results?

No. They are sampled CBSE senior secondary records.

Does affiliation cover every brand branch?

No. Match the exact campus and affiliation number.

Are admission dates and fees fixed here?

No. Use the current grade and session notices.

Are other boards included?

No. CISCE, state boards and IB need separate sources.

How can two nearby schools be compared?

Use actual subjects, support, commute and complete annual costs.
