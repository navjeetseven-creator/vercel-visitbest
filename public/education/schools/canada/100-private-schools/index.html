100 Private Schools in Canada: Directory and Admissions Guide

97 Canadian CAIS-directory schools plus three Ontario private-school records. Not a government-endorsed national ranking.

Private-school choices in Canada need provincial context. This guide combines 97 Canadian school records from the CAIS directory with three additional schools from Ontario’s official private-school contact list. The sample includes different grade ranges and school models. Membership, a government contact-list entry and authority to grant diploma credits are different facts. The table records each source so a family can check the appropriate current policy instead of treating every listing as the same kind of approval.

Sources and selection method

All 97 Canadian CAIS records, then 3 non-duplicate Ontario records selected by location/alphabetical round-robin.

Not all schools are secondary. Membership and government listing are not quality endorsement; verify diploma-credit authority.

Source: CAIS directory and Ontario private-school contact list. Source records checked 2026-10-01. Current admissions, courses and fees require the institution’s own current evidence. Each row links to its source.

Start with the province and grade

Education requirements and qualifications differ between provinces. Check the intended school’s grade range before considering its facilities or examination claims. Some directory entries include primary education and others extend through secondary school. Ask how the school places pupils arriving from another province or country. A familiar school name does not establish that it offers the grade, campus or qualification needed by the child.

Distinguish listing from credit authority

Ontario’s private-school contact list describes schools and whether they report offering OSSD credits. A listing is not a government endorsement of quality. For a secondary qualification, verify the current authority and actual credits delivered by the school. CAIS membership is a separate organizational record. The guide does not infer that membership proves every course or student-support claim. Keep each evidence type separate in your comparison.

Review admissions and support

Ask about assessment, references, interview requirements and available places for the intended intake. Learning support, language provision and accessibility need a conversation about the child’s needs. Confirm who provides support and what is charged separately. A school that admits international pupils may still impose specific residence or custodianship conditions. Verify those conditions through the school and the relevant authority before making a non-refundable payment.

Calculate the total family cost

Tuition is one part of the bill. Compare registration, uniforms, devices, transport, trips, meals and activities. Where boarding exists, ask for a separate residential quotation. Record the term or academic year covered, the payment schedule and the refund policy. Financial assistance should be treated as confirmed only when the award and renewal conditions are in writing. The guide does not publish invented fee ranges.

Use visits to test the learning environment

Ask how teachers communicate progress and how the school responds when a pupil needs extra help. Compare class organization and subject availability for the intended grade. Read the latest applicable inspection or school review and any response. Ask about moving to the next stage, including transcript transfer and diploma completion. A useful shortlist balances provision, qualification and sustainable cost.

Decision checklist

  • Choose the province and grade
  • Separate membership from approval
  • Verify diploma-credit authority
  • Ask about support charges
  • Compare the full annual cost

Shortlist worksheet

For three feasible options, record the exact programme or year group, current eligibility, full cost, support provision, travel and the official evidence date. Leave a missing answer unresolved instead of estimating it.

Questions before you decide

Are these the top 100 by examination performance?

No. This is a sourced directory selection.

Does Ontario list status mean endorsement?

No. A contact-list record is not a quality endorsement.

Do all entries offer secondary education?

No. Grade ranges vary and are displayed where the source supplies them.

Are all entries boarding schools?

No. Boarding must be confirmed separately.

Is every school authorized to grant OSSD credits?

No. Check the specific current Ontario record and the school’s actual qualification provision.
