Top 100 International Schools in Asia: Cambridge Directory Guide

Seven-country sample of Cambridge directory schools with International and School in their name. Not all Asian international schools.

International schools can follow very different curricula and admission rules. This guide provides 100 Cambridge-directory school records across China, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and the United Arab Emirates. The UAE is included as part of West Asia. The selection uses names containing both “International” and “School” and a published school website. That reproducible naming rule narrows the sample; it does not define every institution that offers an international education.

Verify the exact curriculum and stage

A Cambridge directory entry does not establish that every Cambridge qualification or year group is offered. Ask the campus which primary, lower-secondary, IGCSE or International A Level subjects are available for the intended intake. A school may also teach another curriculum, but that requires its own evidence. Do not infer International Baccalaureate authorization from Cambridge registration. Record the awarding body, examinations and progression route in the comparison worksheet.

Check admission eligibility before paying

Nationality, residence, age and prior qualifications can affect access in different destinations. Ask the school for the applicable policy and confirm any government requirement through an official source. A published website does not guarantee places for all applicants. Confirm whether an assessment or language review is needed and whether a place is conditional. Keep the admission entity and campus address visible on any deposit request.

Compare language and learning support

English-language marketing is not enough to establish the language used in every classroom. Ask about subject teaching, additional-language support and the home-language options available. Learning-support availability should be checked against the child’s actual needs. Ask who assesses needs, what staffing is available and whether support costs extra. A campus with attractive facilities may still offer a different support arrangement from another branch of the same brand.

Use the full fee schedule

The first-year bill can include application, assessment, enrolment, tuition, transport, meals, devices, trips and examinations. Record the academic year and currency and separate refundable deposits from other charges. Ask about future annual increases and mid-year withdrawal terms. Compare the same year group at each school. Scholarships or sibling discounts should be recorded only when the school confirms the amount and conditions for that applicant.

Evaluate the transition to the next destination

Mobile families need records that will help the child move between education systems. Ask how subject choices, transcripts and predicted grades are documented. Older pupils need the exact qualification combinations required by their intended universities. A directory entry cannot guarantee university recognition everywhere. Review the daily commute, term dates and transfer support alongside the curriculum so the shortlist fits the family’s relocation timetable.

Decision checklist

  • Identify the exact campus
  • Verify the awarding body
  • Check applicant eligibility
  • Request the complete fee schedule
  • Plan the next school transition

Shortlist worksheet

For three feasible options, record the exact programme or year group, current eligibility, full cost, support provision, travel and the official evidence date. Leave a missing answer unresolved instead of estimating it.

Questions before you decide

Are all Asian countries represented?

No. The sample covers the seven countries stated in the methodology.

Are all entries IB schools?

No. The source is Cambridge’s directory. IB status must be verified separately.

Does International in the name prove expatriate eligibility?

No. Nationality and residence rules require separate checks.

Are the numbers performance ranks?

No. They identify rows in a geographic sample.

Does the directory confirm every subject?

No. Confirm subjects and stages with the exact campus.
