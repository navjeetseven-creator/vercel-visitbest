Top 100 Large US Finance Programs: Data and Admissions Guide

IPEDS bachelor Finance, General completions. Program size is not teaching quality, placement or salary.

A top-100 finance search often mixes business-school reputation, specialist courses and employment claims. This guide uses a reproducible quantity: reported bachelor completions in Finance, General. It joins NCES IPEDS C2024_A with HD2024 and orders qualifying active institutions by completions. The period is July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2024. The table helps students identify an established programme pipeline. It does not prove the best instruction, a guaranteed internship or a higher salary.

Sources and selection method

CIP 52.0801 Finance, General; award level 5 bachelor; first major; active institution; positive completions; descending CTOTALT.

Completion volume, not quality or employment. Broader business, accounting, economics and graduate-only programmes are excluded.

Source: NCES IPEDS 2024 HD and C2024_A. Source records checked 2026-10-01. Current admissions, courses and fees require the institution’s own current evidence. Each row links to its source.

Understand the NCES filter

The selection uses CIP code 52.0801, bachelor award level and first major records with positive total completions. Finance concentrations reported under broader business codes can be absent. Specialized finance codes are outside this extract. The number counts completed awards during the historical period. It is not current enrollment or available admission places. A large programme and a smaller programme can offer different experiences, but size alone does not establish either experience.

Read the finance curriculum

Students can compare accounting, statistics, economics, corporate finance, investments and quantitative requirements. Programming and mathematics demands can differ. An asset-management route can differ from corporate finance or financial planning. The catalogue needs to show prerequisites and how often electives run. Practical work can include spreadsheets, cases, coding or supervised projects. Applicants need to verify who can participate. Professional-certification claims need the certification body’s current rules.

Assess career evidence carefully

Employment statistics need the graduating cohort, response rate, definition and reporting interval. A small self-selected salary survey is different from a whole-cohort result. Students need finance-major data where available rather than assuming all-business outcomes apply. Career services can offer advising and employer events without guaranteeing a job. International students also need official work-authorization guidance. The table’s completion count supplies no placement or internship evidence.

Check access to the major and net cost

Some students enter finance directly. Others apply after an initial business curriculum. A university offer can have different conditions from admission to a restricted major. Transfer applicants need credit evaluation. Current cost-of-attendance pages distinguish in-state, out-of-state and international categories. Housing, insurance and programme charges complete the budget. Net cost needs aid actually available to the applicant. Renewal conditions belong beside the award amount.

Decision checklist

  • Confirm entry into the major
  • Read quantitative requirements
  • Ask for cohort outcome evidence
  • Compare actual net cost
  • Treat size as one signal

Shortlist worksheet

For three feasible options, record the exact programme or year group, current eligibility, full cost, support provision, travel and the official evidence date. Leave a missing answer unresolved instead of estimating it.

Questions before you decide

Is this a teaching-quality ranking?

No. It orders qualifying programmes by reported completions.

Why can a famous school be absent?

Its finance course may use another CIP code or campus record.

Are MBA programmes included?

No. The filter is bachelor-level awards.

Does the count show this year’s places?

No. It describes completed awards in the stated period.

How can placements be compared?

Use the same cohort, reporting interval, response rate and employment definition.
