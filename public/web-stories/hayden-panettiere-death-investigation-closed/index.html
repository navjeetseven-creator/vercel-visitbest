16 August 2026

Found Unresponsive in Greenville, Aged 36

Panettiere, who was 36, died on 16 August 2026 in Greenville, South Carolina. She was found unresponsive at the apartment where she was staying, a 911 call reported a cardiac arrest, and first responders were unable to revive her that afternoon.

The coroner's finding

Coroner Had Already Ruled the Death an Accidental Overdose

In September, the Greenville County Coroner's Office said Panettiere died from the toxic effects of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, alprazolam, methocarbamol and quetiapine. The coroner's office also said the autopsy found no signs of trauma that contributed to her death.

The wider investigation

A Multi-Agency Investigation That Ran for Two Months

USA Today and other outlets reported in August that the US Drug Enforcement Administration had joined a multi-agency investigation into Panettiere's death. That investigation has now concluded with the police announcement, while USA Today said it had asked the agency about the current status of its case.
