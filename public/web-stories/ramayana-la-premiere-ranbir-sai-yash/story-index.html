Ramayana: Part 1 · Los Angeles premiere

Ramayana's LA Premiere: Ranbir, Sai Pallavi and Yash Take the Epic to Hollywood

Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana: Part 1 held its grand world premiere at the David Geffen Theatre, inside the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures in Los Angeles. The premiere began at 7 am IST on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, with Ranbir Kapoor, Sai Pallavi and Yash on the red carpet alongside director Tiwari and producer Namit Malhotra.

Release · 5 November 2026

Part 1 Arrives on 5 November; Part 2 Next Diwali

Ramayana: Part 1 releases worldwide on 5 November 2026 in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam, and will be presented in IMAX. Sony Pictures is distributing it globally outside India, with Dharma Productions distributing in the Hindi-speaking market. Part 2 follows at Diwali 2027. The cast also includes Sunny Deol as Hanuman and Ravie Dubey as Lakshman.
