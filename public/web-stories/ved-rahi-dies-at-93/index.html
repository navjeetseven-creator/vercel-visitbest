Ved Rahi Dies at 93

Veteran filmmaker and Dogri writer Ved Rahi has died at the age of 93. He died on Sunday morning, 1 November 2026, at his residence in Thane, Maharashtra. His last rites were performed at the Kopari cremation ground in Thane later that Sunday afternoon.

A Jammu beginning

Born in Jammu in May 1933, Son of a Pioneering Journalist

Rahi was born in Jammu in May 1933. His father, Mulk Raj Saraf, was a pioneering journalist in the region, and Rahi grew up in a family with deep journalistic roots. He began his literary journey in Jammu and established himself as an important voice in Dogri writing.

Literary honours

Sahitya Akademi Award in 1983, Fellowship in 2024

Rahi received the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1983 for his Dogri short-story collection Aale. In 2024 he was honoured with the Sahitya Akademi Fellowship, one of the institution’s highest distinctions. He also received the Rahul Sankrityayan Award and the Kusumagraj Rashtriya Sahitya Puraskar.

Hindi cinema

The Writer Behind More Than 25 Films

Rahi wrote screenplays and dialogues for more than 25 Hindi films. His credits include Yeh Raat Phir Na Aayegi, Pavitra Paapi, Aap Aaye Bahaar Aayi, Beimaan, Mome Ki Gudiya, Sanyasi, Charas and Bezubaan — a career that took him from Jammu’s literary circles to the heart of Bombay cinema.

As a director

From Prem Parbat and Kali Ghata to Veer Savarkar

Rahi later turned director, making films including Prem Parbat, featuring the memorable song “Yeh Dil Aur Unki Nigaahon Ke Saaye”, and Kali Ghata, starring Shashi Kapoor and Rekha. His final directorial venture was Veer Savarkar, released in 2001.

Television legacy

Gul Gulshan Gulfam, the Houseboat Family India Loved

Television audiences remember Rahi above all for Gul Gulshan Gulfam, the much-loved Doordarshan serial that followed the lives of a Kashmiri family living on houseboats. His other television work included Meera, Katha Sagar, Zindagi, Ehsaas and Rishtey, bringing his storytelling into households across the country through the 1980s and 1990s.
