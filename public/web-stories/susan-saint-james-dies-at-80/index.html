From Rockford to Hollywood

Born Susan Jane Miller on August 14, 1946, in Los Angeles and raised in Rockford, Illinois, she modelled in France and New York before heading to Hollywood at 20. Her first screen role came in the 1966 television movie Fame Is the Name of the Game.

Susan Saint James · 1946–2026

Her Defining Act

On November 28, 2004, a plane crash in Colorado killed her youngest son, Teddy, aged 14; her husband and son Charlie survived. Weeks later, she delivered Teddy's eulogy as a note to each of her children and her husband. Her family called it her defining act: “Even in the deepest grief of her life, she put everyone else first.” She spent two decades comforting other grieving parents.

Susan Saint James · 1946–2026

Family, Fame and Giving Back

She married NBC executive Dick Ebersol in 1981, weeks after they met when she hosted Saturday Night Live. She received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2008, and served on the boards of the Special Olympics and the Telluride Foundation. She is survived by Dick, her four children — Sunshine, Harmony, Charlie and Willie — seven grandchildren, her sister Mary Mercedes Dewey, and her service dog, Lola.

“She Never Lost That Fire”

“She brought that same fire and positivity to everything that followed, and never lost it,” her family said. Susan Saint James — Peggy, Sally, Kate — leaves behind a half-century of warmth on screen, and a family who say she made everyone she met feel like the most important person in the room.
