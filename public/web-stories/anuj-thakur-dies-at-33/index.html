What happened that morning

A Reported Seizure in His Sleep, His Family Told Rupesh

Rupesh told The Times of India that he learned of the death on Friday night through a common friend, then met Anuj’s mother and brother on Saturday. The family told him Anuj had suffered a seizure in his sleep. Anuj was an early riser who woke at 4.30 am for fitness and jogging; that morning he did not get up, and when his mother went to his room after his alarm, she found that he had passed away. The seizure account is reported by the family through Rupesh; no independent medical confirmation of the exact cause has been reported.

Friends Since 2008, Through Show After Show

Rupesh had known Anuj since 2008, when they worked together on their first show, Bandhan Saath Janmon Ka, and went on to work together on several shows after that. He described Anuj as generous, cheerful and helpful, someone who always spoke respectfully to everyone and treated people with dignity.

The comeback he was planning

Met Just 10 Days Earlier, Talking About a Comeback

Rupesh had met Anuj just 10 days before his death, and said he was talking about making a comeback, with his enthusiasm exactly the same as it had been in 2008. The two had projects planned together, Rupesh wrote, and he still remembered witnessing Anuj’s struggles in his initial days and seeing his journey.

His career

From Dhoom Machao Dhoom to Mahisagar

Anuj’s television journey spanned nearly two decades. He debuted in the Disney Channel teen series Dhoom Machao Dhoom, then moved into mainstream Hindi daily soaps with Bandhan Saath Janmon Ka and Beend Banoongaa Ghodi Chadhunga. His career reached a high point when he played the lead role of Sagar Mehta in the BIG Magic sitcom Mahisagar.
