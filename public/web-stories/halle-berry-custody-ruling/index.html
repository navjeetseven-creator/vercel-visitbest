Halle Berry Custody Ruling: Restraining Order Dropped, But Primary Custody Stays With Ex-Husband for Now

A Los Angeles Superior Court judge has ruled in Halle Berry's custody case with her ex-husband, Olivier Martinez. Judge Shelley Kaufman dissolved the temporary restraining order against Berry, but left primary physical custody of their teenage son with Martinez for now.

Two Parts to the Ruling — and Both Sides Claim a Win

The ruling has two distinct parts, and each side has claimed a win from it. Berry's lawyer called the dissolved restraining order a win for Berry, while Martinez's lawyer, speaking to TMZ as reported by USA TODAY, called primary physical custody a huge victory for his client.

What the judge decided

No Overnight Visits for Now; Joint Legal Custody Continues

Berry and Martinez will continue to share legal custody, according to Page Six and USA TODAY. The judge temporarily denied Berry's requests for equal 50/50 physical custody and for overnight visits, so the custody arrangement put in place with the temporary order stays in place for now.

How the case reached court

A Temporary Order on 1 October, Then a Nine-Day Hearing

Martinez was granted a temporary protective order on 1 October, after alleging that Berry had physically abused their son during an incident on 26 September. Berry asked the judge to deny a permanent restraining order and lift the temporary restrictions. Much of the nine-day hearing that followed was held in closed court.

Berry's position

Berry Denies the Allegations in Full

Berry has consistently denied the allegations, Page Six and USA TODAY report. Her lawyer, Marina Beck, previously called Martinez's filing entirely meritless and deliberately misleading, and argued that Martinez had repeatedly violated court orders and interfered with their son's therapeutic and educational care. Those are her lawyer's arguments, not findings of the court.

Martinez's position

Martinez Sought Sole Custody; His Lawyer Welcomed the Outcome

Martinez had sought sole legal and physical custody. His lawyer told reporters after the ruling that his client had been granted primary physical custody, that Berry had been granted no overnight visits, and that the child was safe in his client's custody. Berry's lawyer, by contrast, said the court dissolving the restraining order was a win for Berry.

The history

Married in 2013, Divorce Finalised in 2023

Berry, 60, and Martinez, 60, married in July 2013 and separated two years later. They were declared legally single in December 2016, and their divorce was finalised in August 2023. That settlement provided for joint legal and physical custody and for Martinez to receive 8,000 dollars a month in child support, USA TODAY reports.

What happens next

An Interim Ruling: Custody to Be Revisited in November

This is an interim ruling, not a final custody settlement: the judge is expected to revisit the custody arrangement in November, according to reports citing TMZ.
