Barbara Barrie · 1931–2026

Barbara Barrie Dies at 95: The Breaking Away and Barney Miller Star

Oscar-nominated actress Barbara Barrie has died at the age of 95. She died on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, at her home in Manhattan, after a terminal cancer diagnosis.

Broadway · Company

The Stage Came First — and Company Made Her a Tony Nominee

Born Barbara Ann Berman in Chicago on 23 May 1931 and raised in Corpus Christi, Texas, Barrie made her Broadway debut in The Wooden Dish in 1955. In 1970 she originated the role of Sarah in Stephen Sondheim's Company, earning a Tony nomination in 1971.

Breakthrough · 1964

One Potato, Two Potato Won Her Cannes' Best Actress Prize

Her first leading film role came in 1964's One Potato, Two Potato, in which she played a divorced mother whose remarriage becomes the focus of a custody dispute. Her performance won the Best Actress award at the Cannes Film Festival.

Breaking Away · 1979

Evelyn Stoller Brought Her an Oscar Nomination

Her best-known film role was Evelyn Stoller, the warm-hearted mother in 1979's Breaking Away. The performance earned her an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress, and she later reprised the role in the television series, earning an Emmy nomination.

Television · Barney Miller and Suddenly Susan

Television Audiences Knew Her as Barney's Wife — and Susan's Nana

On television, Barrie played Elizabeth, the wife of Hal Linden's Captain Barney Miller, on Barney Miller between 1975 and 1978. Later generations knew her as Helen 'Nana' Keane, Brooke Shields' grandmother, on the sitcom Suddenly Susan.

Author and advocate

She Wrote About Cancer So Others Would Not Face It Alone

Beyond acting, Barrie wrote two children's books, Lone Star and Adam ZigZag, and two books about her own experience of colorectal cancer, Second Act and Don't Die of Embarrassment. She became a passionate advocate for early detection, and her final Broadway appearance came in Significant Other in 2017.
