Heidi Klum's Severed-Hand Halloween: Her Wildest Costume Yet at 25 Years

Heidi Klum, 53, has revealed her 2026 Halloween costume: a giant, severed replica of her own left hand. She arrived at her 25th annual Halloween party at Cathédrale Restaurant at Moxy East Village in New York on Saturday, 31 October 2026, in a look that left her almost unrecognisable.

The costume in detail

Her Own Left Hand, Writ Large

This was not just any hand. Klum set out to recreate her own left hand, down to its smallest details: long red nails, realistic veins and muscles, a bloody wrist, and her signature jewellery, including her engagement ring and wedding band.

Where is Heidi in there?

Only Her Eyes and Mouth Show

Klum's body sits inside the hand, with her legs forming two of the fingers. Only her eyes and mouth show through an opening at the centre of the hand, with her head positioned at its centre, as the Associated Press and Page Six describe it.

How it was made

An Idea From Last Year, Months in the Making

Klum had the idea last year, telling reporters she looked at her hand and thought she could fit her body inside it somehow. Her team then worked on the costume for months, and getting into it on the night took about five to six hours, with many people helping her into it.

The team behind it

Mike Marino and the Prosthetics Team

The costume was built with longtime collaborator and special-effects artist Mike Marino of ProRenFX, who has worked with Klum on her elaborate transformations for years. The team used her body as the model, so the hand carries her palm lines, veins, rings and even the tiny tattoo on her finger.

The entrance

Flanked by Hazmat Suits

Tom Kaulitz

A Little Bit of Tom, Even in Absence

Her husband, musician Tom Kaulitz, did not attend this year because he was on tour. He still appears in the costume: the hand carries his name in a tattoo on its ring finger, along with her palm lines and veins, and her green-stone wedding ring.

Talk to the Hand — and 25 Years of Halloween
