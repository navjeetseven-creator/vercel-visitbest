Deep Purple Postpone Two German Shows as Ian Gillan, 81, Is Hospitalised

Deep Purple have postponed two shows on their German tour after frontman Ian Gillan, 81, was hospitalised following a medical emergency. The band announced the decision on Saturday, 31 October 2026, hours before they were due on stage in Leipzig.

What the band said

A Medical Emergency, in the Band's Own Words

In a statement shared on Saturday, the band said: “This comes as a result of a medical emergency involving Ian Gillan, which has resulted in his current hospitalisation.” The band said it was announcing the changes “with great disappointment” and apologised for the short notice.

The two shows affected

Leipzig Tonight, Hamburg Tomorrow

The shows affected are tonight, Saturday 31 October, at the QI Arena — the Quarterback Immobilien Arena — in Leipzig, and tomorrow evening, Sunday 1 November, at the Sporthalle in Hamburg. Both dates are on the German leg of the band's current tour.

Who is Ian Gillan?

Deep Purple's Voice Since 1969

Gillan, 81, has been Deep Purple's lead singer since 1969, when he joined the band after leaving Episode Six, replacing original frontman Rod Evans. He has fronted the band ever since his return in 1992, and remains its lead singer and lyricist.

The tour

The SPLAT! World Tour, Running Since August

The postponed dates are part of Deep Purple's SPLAT! world tour, which has been running since early August 2026, with performances scheduled through December. The current line-up alongside Gillan is guitarist Simon McBride, bassist Roger Glover, drummer Ian Paice and keyboardist Don Airey.
