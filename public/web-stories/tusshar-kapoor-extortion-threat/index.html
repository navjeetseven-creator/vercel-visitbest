Tusshar Kapoor Gets a ₹5 Crore Extortion Threat

Bollywood actor Tusshar Kapoor has received an extortion threat demanding ₹5 crore from a person claiming to be gangster Hari Boxer, police sources told multiple outlets on 3 November 2026. Kapoor approached the local police after receiving the threat, and the case was handed to the Mumbai Crime Branch.
