Margaret Qualley and Jack Antonoff File for Divorce After Three Years of Marriage

Margaret Qualley, 32, and Jack Antonoff, 42, have officially filed for divorce. The pair, who married in August 2023, separated earlier this year after nearly three years of marriage, PEOPLE and Page Six reported on Friday, 30 October 2026.

A joint, uncontested filing

Filed Jointly in New York, With a Settlement Agreement

“Jack and Margaret are filing for divorce jointly,” a source close to the pair tells PEOPLE. “This is a mutual decision.” Qualley was listed as the plaintiff in the New York filing, Page Six reported from court records — New York requires a plaintiff and a defendant to be listed — and a settlement agreement has also been filed and is still being processed by the court.

The Separation Was Confirmed in July — With a Privacy Request
