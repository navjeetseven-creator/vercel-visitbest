Manish Plays Qazi's Message

Manish Goel goes first, Times Now and TellyChakkar both report. He is handed Qazi Touqeer's family message and chooses to play it rather than take an extra lifeline. Qazi hears his parents' voices and gets emotional, and also receives a framed photograph featuring him and his mother, TellyChakkar reports. His brother assures him everything at home is being taken care of and encourages him to focus on winning the trophy; his mother sends Diwali wishes to all the contestants and says she hopes to see him lift the trophy.
