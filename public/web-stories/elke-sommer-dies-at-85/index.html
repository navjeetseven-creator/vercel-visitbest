Elke Sommer · 1940–2026

Elke Sommer Dies at 85: The Pink Panther Star, a Week Before Her 86th Birthday

Golden Globe-winning actress Elke Sommer has died at the age of 85. She died on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, at her home in the Bel Air neighbourhood of Los Angeles, of heart failure — one week before her 86th birthday.

Her Stepson Shared the News With Bild

Her stepson, Sebastian Walther, told the German newspaper Bild that Sommer “passed away peacefully around 10 p.m.” on Wednesday. “Now she can finally be with her beloved husband again,” he added. Her husband of 32 years, hotelier Wolf Walther, died of cancer in July 2026.

Berlin · 1940

Six Languages, a Beauty Contest — and a Chance Discovery

Born in Berlin in November 1940, Sommer first planned to become an interpreter and spoke six languages as a teenager. While on holiday in Italy in 1958, she won the Miss Viareggio beauty contest at 17, and director Vittorio De Sica spotted her — the start of a film career.

Breakthrough · The Prize

Paul Newman, The Prize — and a Golden Globe

After Italian and German films in the early 1960s, Sommer landed her Hollywood breakthrough opposite Paul Newman in The Prize (1963). The role won her the Golden Globe for Most Promising Newcomer in 1964.

Maria Gambrelli, Opposite Peter Sellers' Clouseau

Marriages and later years

Two Marriages, and a Life Beyond the Screen

Sommer married Hollywood columnist Joe Hyams in 1964; they divorced in 1981. She met hotelier Wolf Walther in 1989 while performing in the play Tamara in New York, and they were married for 32 years. In recent years she had stepped away from the spotlight.
