Michael Mann Taken to Hospital After Gun-Range Accident in Los Angeles

Director Michael Mann, 83, was taken to hospital after accidentally shooting himself in the leg at a gun range in Los Angeles, TMZ reports. Los Angeles police confirmed to TMZ that officers and firefighters responded to The Range LA after reports of a shooting.

Who is Michael Mann

The Chicago-Born Director Behind the Crime Drama

Michael Kenneth Mann was born on 5 February 1943 in Chicago, Illinois. He is one of Hollywood's most acclaimed filmmakers, known for stylish, intense crime dramas and an elegantly stylised realism, as Britannica and Parade describe his work.

Beyond Heat

Miami Vice, The Insider, Collateral and More

Mann was executive producer of the television series Miami Vice (1984-90). He directed Daniel Day-Lewis in The Last of the Mohicans (1992), Russell Crowe and Pacino in The Insider (1999), which earned seven Academy Award nominations, Tom Cruise and Jamie Foxx in Collateral (2004), Will Smith in Ali (2001) and Johnny Depp in Public Enemies (2009).

Ferrari, Heat 2, and a Career of Major Honours
