Delhi Court Pulls Up Nora Fatehi Over Court Absence

Delhi's Rouse Avenue Court on Monday, 2 November 2026, expressed displeasure after actor Nora Fatehi did not appear as scheduled to testify as a prosecution witness in the Rs 200 crore extortion case linked to alleged conman Sukesh Chandrashekhar. The court issued fresh summons directing her to appear on 6 November 2026.

What the court said

“Her Conduct Is Not Up To The Mark,” Court Observed

Fatehi's lawyer sought exemption from her appearance, prompting the court to caution that the proceedings could not be taken lightly. As reported by The Economic Times (PTI/ANI) and ABP Live (ANI), the court observed: “Her conduct is not up to the mark. Don't take the court for granted,” adding, as ABP Live reports, “She is a witness and was supposed to be here.”

An important distinction

Fatehi Is A Witness, Not An Accused

Fatehi has been summoned as a prosecution witness — not as an accused — in the case, Times Now reported on 30 October, citing ANI. She is listed as witness LW-3 in the third supplementary chargesheet; a listed witness becomes a prosecution witness on appearing in court. Nothing in today's hearing makes her an accused in the case.

How the summons came about

Summons Ordered On 16 October For 2 November

In an order dated 16 October 2026, Additional Sessions Judge Gagandeep Singh directed that summons be issued to Fatehi for 2 November, The Economic Times and The Times of India reported. Delhi Police told the court notice was served on 22 October via email, WhatsApp and phone calls, ABP Live and NewsBytes reported, while her counsel told the court she was informed on 29 October, The Economic Times reported. Both accounts are reported as reported.

The case behind the hearing

The Alleged Rs 200 Crore Extortion Case

The case concerns allegations that Chandrashekhar, while lodged in Delhi's Rohini Jail in 2020-21, impersonated senior government officials and extorted around Rs 200 crore from Aditi Singh, wife of former Ranbaxy promoter Shivinder Mohan Singh, promising to secure her husband's release from jail. Delhi Police's Economic Offences Wing investigated the allegations and filed a chargesheet under the Maharashtra Control of Organised Crime Act (MCOCA).

Who is Nora Fatehi

Dancer, Actor And Singer

Nora Fatehi is a Canadian singer, dancer and actress based in India. She entered the Bigg Boss house as a wild-card contestant in its ninth season in 2015, rose to wide fame with the recreated song “Dilbar” in Satyameva Jayate, and has since judged dance shows including Dance Deewane Juniors and Jhalak Dikhhla Jaa 10.
