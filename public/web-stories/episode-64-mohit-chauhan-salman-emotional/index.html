Why This Song Matters

The song comes from the 1963 film Bachpan, in which Salim Khan appeared as an actor before he became one half of the Salim-Javed screenwriting duo. TellyChakkar reports it was sung by Mohammed Rafi, composed by Sardar Malik and written by Hasrat Jaipuri. TellyChakkar also cautions that the promo does not reveal exactly why Salman became emotional — whether it was his father's early acting days or a personal memory — so the reason is not confirmed here.
