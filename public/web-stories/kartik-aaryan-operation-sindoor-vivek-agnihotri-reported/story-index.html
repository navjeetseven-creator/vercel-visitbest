The source material · The book

Based on Lt. Gen. Dhillon's Book

The film is based on Lt. Gen. K. J. S. 'Tiny' Dhillon's book Operation Sindoor: The Untold Story of India's Deep Strikes Inside Pakistan, Variety India reports. Agnihotri says the film will explore an operation that, in his words, redefined security in the subcontinent.

Agnihotri's March announcement

“A Story Drawn from Reality”

Announcing the project on social media in March, Agnihotri wrote that it was “rooted in the aftermath of the Pahalgam terror attack and backed by extensive, ground-level research in collaboration with multiple wings of the Indian Armed Forces”, adding: “this is a story drawn from reality… not to create noise, but to confront it: with facts, with clarity, and the magic of cinema.”

Kartik's slate · What's next

Naagzilla, Anurag Basu and Kabir Khan Films Follow

Kartik will next be seen in Naagzilla, slated for 12 February 2027, produced by Karan Johar and Mahaveer Jain and directed by Mrighdeep Singh Lamba. An untitled musical romantic drama with Sreeleela, directed by Anurag Basu, follows on 30 April 2027, and he has reunited with Kabir Khan for a biographical sports drama on kickboxing champion Tajamul Islam. Bhool Bhulaiyaa 4 and Kaaliya are also in the pipeline, Variety India reports.
