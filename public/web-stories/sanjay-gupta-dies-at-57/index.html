What happened

A Fall at Home, Confirmed by Friends and Police

Gupta died after a fall at his Mumbai home in which he suffered a head injury. Filmmaker Ashoke Pandit confirmed the death on social media, saying Gupta had passed due to a head injury at home. Close friend Milap Zaveri told PTI: “It is true that he passed away. He fell and hit his head.” Versova police registered an accidental death report and, on preliminary information, found nothing suspicious.

Born 23 October 1969

A Birthday Just Days Earlier

Gupta was born on 23 October 1969, and had celebrated his birthday just days before his death. He is survived by his wife, Anuradha Lekhi, and their children, Shivansh and Dalai. Details of his last rites were still awaited when the news broke.

A career in crime cinema

From Aatish in 1994 to a Signature Style

Gupta made his directorial debut with Aatish: Feel the Fire in 1994, starring Sanjay Dutt and Aditya Pancholi. Over three decades he built a distinctive place in Hindi cinema with crime dramas, urban thrillers and stylised action, often through his production banner, White Feather Films.

The breakthrough

Kaante (2002): Six Men, One Heist

His breakthrough was Kaante (2002), which he directed and produced. The heist thriller brought together Amitabh Bachchan, Sanjay Dutt, Suniel Shetty, Mahesh Manjrekar, Lucky Ali and Kumar Gaurav, and went on to develop a cult following for its gritty, ensemble storytelling.

The underworld years

Musafir, Zinda and the Shootout Films

Gupta followed Kaante with Musafir (2004) and Zinda (2006). He produced and co-wrote Shootout at Lokhandwala (2007), directed by Apoorva Lakhia, and returned to the director’s chair for Shootout at Wadala (2013) — films that cemented his name in Hindi cinema’s underworld cycle.

Later work

Jazbaa, Kaabil and Mumbai Saga

His later directorial work included Jazbaa (2015), starring Aishwarya Rai Bachchan, Kaabil (2017), with Hrithik Roshan as a blind dubbing artist, and Mumbai Saga (2021), featuring John Abraham and Emraan Hashmi. His most recent directorial work was The Miranda Brothers, starring Harshvardhan Rane and Meezaan Jafri.
