Bigg Boss 20 Crosses 100 Crore Watch Hours — A First in 20 Years

A First in 20 Years

Bigg Boss 20 has crossed 100 crore watch hours on JioHotstar in under 60 days — the first season in the franchise's history to reach the mark. News18, Outlook India (carrying the PTI report) and the Free Press Journal all report the same figure, announced by the streaming platform on Friday 6 November.
