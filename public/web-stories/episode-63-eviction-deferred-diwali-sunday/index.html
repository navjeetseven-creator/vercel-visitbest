No Eviction on Saturday

Salman Khan closed Episode 63, aired on Saturday 7 November, without an eviction — and told the housemates he will announce the result in the next episode, on Diwali Sunday 8 November. Times Now's highlights and JustShowBiz's written update both report the same ending. An earlier report had said there would be no elimination this weekend at all; the aired episode showed the result is deferred to Sunday, not skipped.

Love's Retort

Love Gill objected when Gaurav joked that Love gives Scout free 'massage therapies', telling him he needs therapy instead and saying, 'I'm not going to take this.' The Times of India reports that Gaurav then dragged Rohit Shetty and Salman Khan into the argument, saying Love does not listen even to them — and Love replied, 'I respect them, but you're taking this topic elsewhere.' Gaurav apologised to the contestants as he ended his act.
