How the 2X Room Vote Worked

Bigg Boss introduced the 2X Room to give Arishfa another opportunity, both outlets report. The contestants had to choose between two doors: the 2 Door meant support for Arishfa's captaincy, and the X Door meant they did not want her to become captain. Nine contestants are named by both outlets as choosing the 2 Door: Qazi Touqeer, Yung DSA, Gullu, Aamrapali Dubey, Kanika Mann, Love Gill, Mahhi Vij, Uditi Singh and Manish Goel. The IndiaForums live-feed discussion adds that Scout OP also backed her, which would make the support unanimous — that last detail rests on the live-feed thread alone and is labelled as such here.
