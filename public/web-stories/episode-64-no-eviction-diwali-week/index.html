No Eviction This Diwali Week: Salman Khan's Diwali Gift to the Housemates

No Eviction This Diwali Week

Salman Khan has given the housemates a Diwali gift: nobody will be evicted this week. TellyExpress's written update of tonight's episode, 8 November 2026, says Salman told the nominated contestants there was no need to announce who received the least votes, as nobody will be evicted on the occasion of Diwali. Deccan Chronicle and Siasat had reported the same outcome ahead of the weekend, and tonight it happened on air.
