The law in the case

MCOCA Provisions Invoked on 27 October

On 27 October 2026, police added charges under the Maharashtra Control of Organised Crime Act (MCOCA), 1999, including Sections 3(1)(ii), 3(2) and 3(4). An earlier probe, as reported by ABP Live, said the alleged mastermind used 25 to 30 bank accounts and digital payment channels.
