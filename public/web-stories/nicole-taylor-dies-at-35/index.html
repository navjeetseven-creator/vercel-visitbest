Taylor Red Singer Nicole Taylor Dies at 35

Taylor Red singer Nicole Taylor has died at 35. Her identical triplet sisters and bandmates, Nika and Natalie Taylor, announced on Monday 2 November 2026 that their sister had died. According to the band's website, as reported by Entertainment Weekly and USA Today, Nicole died on 28 September 2026 at her home in Nashville, Tennessee.

Sisters Share the News in an Emotional Video

Nika and Natalie shared the news in an emotional video posted on 2 November, saying they were heartbroken to announce their sister's passing. They said Nicole was at peace and no longer suffering. The sisters described Nicole, the oldest of the three, as their leader and their best friend.

The diagnosis

Stage 3 Breast Cancer Diagnosed in 2022

Nicole was diagnosed with stage 3 breast cancer in 2022, her sisters said. They described the cancer as a very aggressive type. The news came as a shock to the sisters, who said they learned of the diagnosis while they were out together.

Treatment and hope

She Responded Well at First — and Kept Living Fully

Nicole responded well to treatment at first and was at one point declared to have no evidence of disease, her sisters said. During chemotherapy she even did CrossFit, and she tried not to live in fear, remaining hopeful, travelling and working hard while she could.

The illness returns

Cancer Advanced to Stage 4

After that hopeful period, the cancer returned and advanced to stage 4, Natalie said. At that point Nicole was told she had about three months to live. She went on to live for about another year, her sisters said, holding on to her faith and the peace they said it gave her.

Who Taylor Red are

Identical Triplets From Mississippi, Singing Since Childhood

Nicole, Nika and Natalie Taylor are identical triplets who grew up in South Mississippi playing guitar, banjo, fiddle and mandolin. As Taylor Red, formerly Red Roots, they built a large online following, with more than 13 million followers on TikTok and 16 million subscribers on YouTube, and their 2021 song Country Money became widely known after going viral.
