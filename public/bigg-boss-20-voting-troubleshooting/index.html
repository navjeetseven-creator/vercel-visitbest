Bigg Boss 20 Voting Help: Missing Options and Failed Votes

When an official voting option is missing, first check the round and the instructions shown by the platform. A missing button does not prove that a contestant has been eliminated or that a fan poll can replace the official process.

By VisitBestPrepared September 30, 20263 min read
On this page

  2. Confirm the selected contestant belongs to the round
  3. Read account and access messages literally
  4. For a failed submission, look for confirmation
  5. Update the official app or try a supported browser
  6. Do not replace an unavailable option with a random number

Check whether the current round is open

Voting can be restricted to a stated window. Open the official Season 20 destination and look for the current voting announcement. Compare its deadline and time zone with the current time.

A screenshot from another week can show a button that is no longer available. A guide that gives a permanent Monday-to-Friday schedule can also be wrong when the show changes its process. The active announcement is the reference.

Confirm the selected contestant belongs to the round

The official options can be limited to the nominated participants. A contestant who is not listed may be exempt from that round, absent from the process or shown under another spelling. Read the current nomination announcement before treating the omission as a technical fault.

The nomination explainer explains eligibility, immunity and dated lists. VisitBest’s historical reports do not override a later official announcement.

Read account and access messages literally

If sign-in is required, use the account requested by the official platform. If the interface gives a region or account restriction, follow its help instructions. Access to an episode does not by itself establish every voting entitlement.

Keep one submission window open while troubleshooting. Repeated tabs can make it difficult to know which account or selection is active. Never share a one-time password with a third-party “voting agent.”

For a failed submission, look for confirmation

A tap is not a confirmed vote if the interface reports an error. Read the message, check the connection and retry only according to the displayed instructions. If a limit has been reached, repeated taps will not necessarily create additional valid entries.

The number of permitted votes needs to come from the current rules. This guide does not assume one vote per account or an unlimited allowance. Use the confirmation displayed by the platform as the submission record.

Update the official app or try a supported browser

For a frozen or incomplete interface, update the official app, reopen it and check the show page again. If voting is offered on the official website, a supported browser can help distinguish an app problem from a wider issue.

If the error persists, use the platform’s help route and describe the message, device and time of the attempt. Avoid posting a screenshot that exposes personal account information.

Do not replace an unavailable option with a random number

Missed-call voting is valid only when the show publishes a current number and states how it works. Numbers copied from old seasons or unsourced lists do not establish a Bigg Boss 20 voting method.

The VisitBest poll remains an unofficial opinion poll. The fan-versus-official guide explains the distinction if another page suggests that the two actions are equivalent.
