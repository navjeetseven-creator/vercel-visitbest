Start with the episode, then follow the decision

Bigg Boss 20 is the Hindi edition hosted by Salman Khan. The season began on September 6, 2026. Its official digital destination is JioHotstar, while COLORS carries the television broadcast. The premiere announcement listed 9 PM on JioHotstar and 10:30 PM on COLORS; check the current program listing for later episodes rather than treating launch times as a permanent schedule.

Use the viewing guide to find the official show page. After watching, open the episode guide to separate an episode recap from a short promo or a live-stream clip. The date matters when a task begins in one episode and finishes in another.

Meet the cast beyond the nomination list

The existing contestant directory provides the broader lineup. The new profiles focus on selected participants and their public careers. Read about Mary Kom’s boxing background, Scout’s gaming career or Qazi Touqeer’s return to reality television.

Career recognition and performance inside the house answer different questions. An actor can arrive with years of screen experience and still need to earn trust in a task. A creator can bring a large audience without controlling an official elimination. Profiles explain the starting point; dated coverage records the subsequent decisions.

Understand what the labels mean

A nomination makes someone eligible for a particular elimination process. Captaincy, immunity and task victory are separate statuses. The nomination explainer and captaincy guide explain why the exact announcement matters.

“Safe” can mean exempt from nominations, saved by an announced outcome or simply popular in an unofficial poll. Those meanings are not interchangeable. When a headline uses the word, read the date and the reason before drawing a conclusion.

Choose the right poll for the question

The VisitBest audience poll records visitor preferences. The best-contestant poll asks a different question from the winner-prediction poll. A voter can prefer one housemate’s daily performance and predict that another will win.

VisitBest polls do not submit an official eviction vote. For that distinction, use fan polls versus official voting. Follow the current official platform’s instructions for eligibility, deadlines and submission confirmation.
