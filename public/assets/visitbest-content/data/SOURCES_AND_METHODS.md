# Sources and methods

All 15 tables contain exactly 100 sourced records. Dates identify research checks, not necessarily dataset publication years. Numbers are row identifiers except for the stated CWTS/completion ordering and official NIRF rank field.

## universities-world

Source: CWTS Leiden Ranking Open Edition 2025

Selection: All sciences; world; 2020–2023; fractional counting; core publications only; minimum P=1000; descending P(top 10%).

Limits: One research-volume measure. No teaching, fees, employment or overall university rank.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## universities-europe

Source: CWTS Leiden Ranking Open Edition 2025

Selection: All sciences; Europe; 2020–2023; fractional counting; core publications only; minimum P=1000; descending P(top 10%).

Limits: One research-volume measure. No teaching, fees, employment or overall university rank.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## grammar-schools-uk

Source: https://get-information-schools.service.gov.uk/Downloads

Selection: Open, state-funded, Secondary, Selective; round-robin by local authority; alphabetical within each authority.

Limits: England only. Numbers are not performance ranks.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## high-schools-england

Source: https://get-information-schools.service.gov.uk/Downloads

Selection: Open, state-funded, Secondary, Non-selective; round-robin across English regions; alphabetical within each region.

Limits: England only; no result ranking.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## finance-schools-us

Source: NCES IPEDS 2024 HD and C2024_A

Selection: CIP 52.0801 Finance, General; award level 5 bachelor; first major; active institution; positive completions; descending CTOTALT.

Limits: Completion volume, not quality or employment. Broader business, accounting, economics and graduate-only programmes are excluded.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## high-schools-usa

Source: https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/Data/zip/ccd_sch_029_2324_w_1a_073124.zip

Selection: 2023–24 CCD version 1a; open/new/added/changed regular High schools offering grades 9 and 12; two per state via geographic round-robin.

Limits: 50 states only. Historical directory edition. No performance ranking.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## private-schools-canada

Source: CAIS directory and Ontario private-school contact list

Selection: All 97 Canadian CAIS records, then 3 non-duplicate Ontario records selected by location/alphabetical round-robin.

Limits: Not all schools are secondary. Membership and government listing are not quality endorsement; verify diploma-credit authority.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## high-schools-canada

Source: CAIS directory and Ontario public-school contact list

Selection: 60 Canadian CAIS records with secondary grades and 40 Ontario public secondary records; geographic round-robin.

Limits: Public-school coverage is Ontario only. Provincial qualifications differ; no performance rank.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## schools-australia

Source: NSW Department of Education; Victoria school locations 2025; Queensland NSSAB register

Selection: 34 NSW government secondary/central schools + 33 Victorian open secondary/combined + 33 Queensland open non-state host-centre secondary schools; geographic round-robin.

Limits: Three states only. Sector coverage differs. No NAPLAN, ATAR or ICSEA ranking.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## private-schools-world

Source: GIAS England, CAIS Canada and Victoria school locations

Selection: 35 English independent schools ages extending into secondary + 35 Canadian CAIS schools + 30 Victorian non-government secondary/combined schools.

Limits: Three-country sample only. Different directory definitions; no global quality rank.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## boarding-schools-world

Source: TABS public school directory and GIAS England

Selection: 35 English independent boarding records + all non-US TABS records not duplicating the English selection + US records to reach 100; geographic sampling.

Limits: Coverage concentrated in England and USA. Directory status does not independently verify residential safety.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## international-schools-world

Source: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/why-choose-us/find-a-cambridge-school/

Selection: Country searches; names contain International and School; listed school website; geographic round-robin. Countries: Brazil, China, France, Germany, Indonesia, Japan, Kenya, Malaysia, Mexico, Singapore, South Africa, Switzerland, Thailand, UAE, UK

Limits: Cambridge subset and name filter only; excludes many international schools. Does not establish IB authorization, ownership, eligibility or boarding.

Checked: 2026-10-01

Checked: 2026-10-01

## schools-india

Source: https://saras.cbse.gov.in/SARAS/AffiliatedList/ListOfSchdirReport

Selection: 10 state/UT queries; Senior Secondary Level; published website; 10 records per area through geographic round-robin.

Limits: CBSE only; 10 areas; no national board-wide performance ranking. Recheck affiliation and subjects for the intended intake.

Checked: 2026-10-01

## medical-colleges-india

Source: NIRF 2025 Medical ranking and participant list

Selection: All 50 official ranked institutions, in published rank order; then 50 non-duplicate participants geographically sampled.

Limits: Rows 51–100 are unranked participants, not invented ranks. Not a list of 100 verified MBBS providers; verify course and year with the competent regulator.

Checked: 2026-10-01
